# Asset provenance

The colony buildings, bridges, doors, buttons, loot cases, machinery, terrain, layout, shaders, and scripts were authored for this task. No Marathon game meshes, textures, maps, logos, or audio are bundled. Online Perimeter imagery was used as a layout and art-direction reference only.

## CC0 environment inputs

The following Poly Haven assets are available under [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Derived geometry and textures are bundled; full-resolution sources are reproducible with `source/fetch_nature.py`. Download URLs and checked MD5 values are recorded in `godot/data/nature-sources.json`.

- [Fir Tree 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/fir_tree_01): Rob Tuytel (photography), Rico Cilliers (modeling). Selected C variant, normalized and simplified to approximately 220,000 triangles.
- [Fir Sapling Medium](https://polyhaven.com/a/fir_sapling_medium): Rob Tuytel (photography), Rico Cilliers (modeling). Selected A variant, normalized and simplified to approximately 150,000 triangles.
- [Fern 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02): Rob Tuytel (scanning), Rico Cilliers (modeling).
- [Grass Medium 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_01): Rob Tuytel (photography), Rico Cilliers (modeling). Selected large C clump.
- [Coastal Cliff 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/coastal_cliff_02): Rob Tuytel. Simplified to approximately 9,000 triangles.
- [Rock Moss Set 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_moss_set_01): Kless Gyzen. Six simplified variants reused from the Vesper Reach kit.
- [Rocky Trail](https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_trail): Amal Kumar.
- [Forest Ground 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/forrest_ground_01): Rob Tuytel.
- [Concrete Floor 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_01): Rob Tuytel.
- [Industrial Sunset (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/industrial_sunset_puresky): Sergej Majboroda (original), Jarod Guest (sky edits), 2K HDR panorama.

Blender and Godot are used as development tools; their executables are not redistributed.
